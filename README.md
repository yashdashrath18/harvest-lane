# Harvest Lane

An original 2.5D farming and market tycoon, built with Phaser 3 and
TypeScript. Grow corn and carrots, collect eggs and milk, stock your
market stall, serve customers against their patience, buy upgrades,
unlock a second field, and complete missions for coins and gems.

**Play it here:** https://yashdashrath18.github.io/harvest-lane/

## About this repository

The playable game is served from this repository's root via GitHub
Pages (the `index.html` and `assets/` are the production build). The
full TypeScript source is in `src/`, the design and QA record is in
`PHASE_REPORTS.md`, and platform notes are in `docs/`.

All art is drawn in code and all music and sound effects are
synthesized live with WebAudio. No downloaded assets, no ads, no
in-app purchases.

## Controls

- Drag anywhere: floating virtual joystick
- Tap or click: walk to that point (tap a ripe crop to harvest it)
- WASD or arrow keys to move, E or Space to harvest

Progress autosaves locally in your browser.

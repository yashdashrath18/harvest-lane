# Harvest Lane: game description

## Short description (for listings)

Grow it, harvest it, sell it. Harvest Lane is a cosy farming and
market tycoon: plant corn and carrots, collect eggs and milk, stock
your market stall, and serve the queue of customers before their
patience runs out.

## Long description

Harvest Lane is an original 2.5D farming and market tycoon built for
quick, cheerful play sessions.

You run a small farm with a market stall at its heart. Walk the
fields and harvest ripe corn into your backpack, carry it to the
stall, and watch customers queue up with orders. Serve them before
their patience bars empty and the coins fly. Chickens peck around
their pen laying eggs, cows graze and produce milk, and every
product has its price.

Coins buy real progress: a bigger backpack, faster boots, stronger
fertilizer, and a market charm that lifts your prices. Save up to
unlock the fenced South Field and plant a carrot crop. Six missions
track what you actually do (harvest, sell, serve, collect, unlock)
and pay coin and gem rewards.

Everything is drawn and composed in code: the art, the characters,
and even the music and sound effects are synthesized live, with no
downloaded assets. The game autosaves as you play.

Features
- A full farming loop: grow, harvest, stock, serve, earn, upgrade
- Two crops (corn, carrots), two animal products (eggs, milk)
- Chickens and cows that wander, graze, and produce on a cycle
- Customers with order bubbles and patience timers
- Four upgrade tracks and a second field to unlock
- Six missions with coin and gem rewards
- Synthesized music and sound with independent volume controls
- Touch joystick, tap-to-move, mouse, and keyboard controls
- Autosave with reload recovery
- Portrait play, designed for phones

Contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat, and no user-generated
content. Single player, offline-friendly after load in standalone
mode.

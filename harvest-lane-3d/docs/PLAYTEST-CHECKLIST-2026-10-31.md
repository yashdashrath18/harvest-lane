# Harvest Lane 3D: 5 minute playtest checklist (competition update only)

Game: https://yashdashrath18.github.io/harvest-lane/3d/
Purpose: judge feel on a real phone. The assistant cannot do this part.
Report only what felt slow, confusing, or broken, with a screenshot if
anything looks wrong. Do not retest the whole old loop; test what is new.

1. Joystick and walk-over harvest: does the farmer move smoothly and
   collect a ripe crop just by stepping on it, without sticking on
   fences or gates?
2. Cash pile: after a sale, does money stack beside the stall and
   collect when you walk over it, instead of paying instantly?
3. Animal feeding: fill a trough with corn, then let it run empty.
   Does production stop and does the red "!" marker show clearly?
4. Helper (300 coins) and churn (400 coins): if you can afford either,
   does the helper harvest and stock on his own, and does milk turn
   into cheese that customers then order?
5. Customer waves: do customers arrive in groups of 3, then pause for
   a calm break, instead of an endless stressful queue?
6. Levels and Saved indicator: does the XP bar and level badge move
   when you harvest, collect, and serve, and does "Saved" flash?
7. Daily missions and contracts: does the missions panel show fresh
   daily missions at the top and a contract that replaces itself
   after you claim it?

Skip offline earnings in this pass: it needs at least 15 minutes away
and is capped at 4 hours, so it cannot be judged in 5 minutes.

If the feel is good, the next decisions are: submit 2D or 3D (see
~/workspace/youtube-playables/harvest-lane-3d-submission-update.md),
and whether to build the queued batch (decorations, New Season
prestige, timed Instant Orders, wheat plus flour mill, goats,
sendScore). If the feel is off, fix that before building more.
